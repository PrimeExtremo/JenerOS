# Clients (later phases)

Apps that connect to a JenerOS box. Nothing here yet — see `[DOCS]/ROADMAP.md` Phase 8.

| Client | Platform | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `tv/` | Fire TV / Android TV | Jener TV: movies/shows from the TV app (Jellyfin API), photo slideshows |
| `phone/` | Android / iOS | Photo backup, files, home controls, remote access setup |

Until then: the dashboard works in any phone/TV browser, and the upstream apps
(Immich, Jellyfin, Home Assistant, Nextcloud) have their own clients that work with JenerOS.
