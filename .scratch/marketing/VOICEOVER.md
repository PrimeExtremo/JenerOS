# JenerOS voiceover scripts (for ElevenLabs)

Paste one line at a time into ElevenLabs (or the whole block; the `...` and line breaks give natural pauses). Export each as WAV/MP3 and lay it on the timeline in Premiere at the times shown.

**Voice:** warm, calm, friendly, a little playful. Not a movie-trailer voice; think a friend showing you something cool. Try a few library voices described as "warm", "conversational", "narration".
**Settings to start with:** Stability 50%, Similarity 75%, Style 15-25%, Speaker boost on. Model: the newest multilingual / expressive model available on your plan.
**Pace:** about 2.3 words per second. Each line below fits its slot with room to breathe.
