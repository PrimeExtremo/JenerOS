## Rules that make it look expensive
1. **The camera never stops.** Every shot has a slow push, a dolly or an orbit, and shots are about 1-3 s long.
2. **Real lens behavior.** Shallow DOF on every macro shot, focus pulls (rack focus) as moments in themselves, a small chromatic lens flare on bright edges, and motion blur on fast moves (Blender: Render > Motion Blur).
3. **Materials.** Thick bevelled glass with real refraction and bright edges, soft frosted glass for UI, glossy rounded icons. In our palette: warm amber glass, dark brown frosted panels, cream highlights, #D97757 glow.
4. **Light.** Volumetric haze in the dark scenes, one strong rim/key light per shot, bloom on the brightest edges. The dark-to-bright contrast is the emotional arc.
5. **Motivated transitions only:** a hard cut on a downbeat, a white/warm flash, a whip pan with blur, macro-to-reveal, screen-to-world, or a word morphing into a UI piece. No plain crossfades between unrelated shots.
6. **Color chapters.** Each section owns a background tone (for JenerOS: dark warm brown, then amber/cream for "lives at home", deep orange for storage, soft cream for updates, back to dark for the end).
7. **Typography.** One to three words, huge and centered, soft fade plus a slight rise or blur-in, one word per beat. Words sometimes turn into icons or panels.
8. **End card.** Calm and centered: logo, hold, URL, fade to black.

## JenerOS version (same grammar, our content)
- **Cold open:** dark warm void with haze. The J logo is BUILT from amber glass voxels flowing together, with the camera on a long lens dollying along its bevel. Hard cut on the downbeat.
- **Macro-to-reveal:** skim the glass top bar (clock, search), then pull back to the whole dashboard on an old PC's monitor in a cozy room (screen-to-world).
- **Icon cloud:** our app tiles (Files, App Store, Photos, Backup, Machines) float in zero-g, the camera flies through, and they fly into their dashboard slots.
- **Words** ("Your stuff." "Lives at home.") turn into the CPU ring widget.
- **Phone setup:** the QR code on the box screen gets a macro rack focus, then a whip pan to the phone, where the "Connected" pill grows.
- **Storage:** disks slide into bays, one per beat, in macro with DOF; the mirror bar fills; a warm flash.
- **Updates:** two glass slabs A/B swap in 3D with a check mark; a speed ramp on the swap.
- **App Store:** the tiles cascade in perspective.
- **End:** the old PC on a calm background, the JENEROS wordmark fades in, "Free. Open source. Yours.", the URL, then fade to black.
