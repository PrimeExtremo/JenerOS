## Techniques to use
1. **Story arc:** relatable moment, then the problem (data goes far away), then the twist (it stays home), then features, then recap, then the call to action.
2. **Kinetic words:** one to three words at a time, mixed weights and gradient keywords, letters blurring in. A handwritten word plus an emoji for humor ("for free!").
3. **Real-world warmth:** living rooms, wood, sunlight, plant shadows on walls, hands doing things (plugging in a drive, filming).
4. **UI on real screens:** a monitor in a dark room with heavy DOF; macro shots of glossy icons; cursor clicks and progress rings.
5. **Playful 3D:** hardware or retro computers tumbling or flying past with motion blur; labeled cursors.
6. **Color per section:** white/purple, then dark, then warm orange, then green, then dark gradients.
7. **Recap bento grid,** then "Out now." with the logo.
8. **Pace:** a cut every 1-2 s, with sound effects on every pop.
