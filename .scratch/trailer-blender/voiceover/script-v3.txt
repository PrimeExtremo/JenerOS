Okay... look in your closet. That old PC? It's not junk. It's your new cloud!
Your photos... your files... your movies... all living at home. Not on someone else's computer.
Setting it up? Plug in a USB stick, turn it on... and just scan the code with your phone!
No keyboard. No commands. No account.
Add two drives, and JenerOS keeps two copies of everything. Lose a drive? Nothing's lost.
Updates go onto a second copy of the system. If one ever fails... it just rolls back.
And your own app store is on the way!
Oh, and the best part? No paid tier. No disk limits. No user limits.
It runs on the PC you already have. That's one less computer in a landfill.
JenerOS. Free. Open source. Yours.
